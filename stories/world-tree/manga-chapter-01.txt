# Manga pages to read and to sell (Amazon KDP print book, 6 x 9 inches). The panels are pictures made in Google Flow
# (free, like the frames), or frames already made for the video (01-world-intro.txt), so nothing is made twice. Flow
# Studio lays out the pages itself: slanted panel borders, the parchment caption boxes with the exact words, at print
# size, and one PDF of all the pages.
#
#  Check it (free):                 .\flow-video.bat check stories\world-tree\manga-chapter-01.txt
#  0. The video's frames first (free): .\flow-video.bat make stories\world-tree\01-world-intro.txt --account 2
#  1. Panels and pages (free):      .\flow-video.bat make stories\world-tree\manga-chapter-01.txt --account 2
#     Redo a panel (free):          .\flow-video.bat make stories\world-tree\manga-chapter-01.txt --account 2 --redo panel 3
#     Fix a caption or the layout:  .\flow-video.bat make stories\world-tree\manga-chapter-01.txt --redo pages
#  The pages: output\world-tree-manga-chapter-01\pages\page-01.png ...
#  The book:  output\world-tree-manga-chapter-01\world-tree-manga-chapter-01.pdf
#
# How a page is written:
#   page 1: 1 tall, 1, 1, 1, 2 tall    the rows from top to bottom: how many panels side by side (1 to 4),
#                                      "tall" or "short" for a taller or shorter row
#   panel 3: <what the picture shows>  a new picture, made in Flow; or  panel 1: frame 1  (a frame of the video)
#   Caption (right): "..."             a caption box: left or right, top or bottom (the first one: top left)
#   Jack (happy, left): "..."          a speech bubble, its tail toward the left
#   focus: top                         which part of the picture to keep when it is cut to the panel (top, middle, bottom)
#
